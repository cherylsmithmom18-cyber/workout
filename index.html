My Workout — Beginner Full-Body Plan

3 days/week with a rest day between (e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri). Goals: glutes, legs, cardio, chest & upper-body tone.

Warm-up (5 min, every day)

3 min easy bike or marching → 10 bodyweight squats (to a chair is fine) → 10 glute bridges → 10 arm circles each way → 10 hip hinges.

Cool-down stretch (5 min)

Hold each 30 sec, breathe slowly, no bouncing: hamstring stretch • figure-4 glute stretch • kneeling/standing hip-flexor stretch • quad stretch (hold support) • doorway chest stretch • child's pose or cat-cow.

Rest, weights & progress

  • Rest 60–90 sec between sets (90 for squats, deadlifts, lunges).
  • Starting weight: the last 2 reps feel challenging but your form stays clean. Can't get 10 good reps? Go lighter. 12 feels easy? Go up next time.
  • Progress weekly: when you hit 3 × 12 with good form, add the smallest step (2.5–5 lb). Weeks 1–2: 2 sets is fine while you learn.
  • Cardio: add 1–2 min or a little incline/resistance each week.
  • Mild soreness for 1–2 days is normal. Sharp or joint pain = stop.
